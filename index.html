What is a Smart & Sustainable City?

A smart city uses technology and eco-friendly systems to improve transport, energy, safety and environment.

Electric buses and AI traffic systems reduce congestion and pollution.

Smart sensors monitor roads and prevent accidents.

Solar panels and wind turbines reduce fossil fuel use.
