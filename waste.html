Smart Waste Management

Smart cities use technology to reduce waste, increase recycling, and protect the environment. Sensors, automation, and data help cities manage waste efficiently and sustainably.

Introduction

Waste management is a major challenge in growing cities. Smart waste systems use sensors, IoT, and data analytics to monitor bins, optimize collection routes, and reduce landfill waste. These solutions help cities become cleaner and more environmentally friendly.

Why Waste Management Must Become Smart

Traditional waste systems are inefficient and costly. Smart technology improves recycling, reduces pollution, and ensures cleaner public spaces.
